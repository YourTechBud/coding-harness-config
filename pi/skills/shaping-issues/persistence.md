# Persistence

Read the repository's `docs/issue-tracking-guidance.md` completely before drafting persisted artifacts. It is the source of truth for the tracker, formats, metadata, relationships, tools, and publication behavior; apply it while preserving this skill's epic and story semantics.

## Configure Repository Guidance

When setup is requested, or guidance is missing when persistence is requested, establish the mapping with the user and save the approved guidance to `docs/issue-tracking-guidance.md` before publishing issues. Resolve existing conventions from repository context and ask about choices that remain unsettled.

Cover what a fresh agent needs to retrieve and maintain the work:

- Where epics and stories live, their identifiers, formats, acceptance criteria, story kinds, and lifecycle metadata.
- Navigation between each epic and its stories, dependencies, and the connection between spikes and the decisions or existing work they affect.
- How explicitly requested candidate epics are stored and later activated.
- How to revise story sets and scope, record findings, and complete or remove artifacts while preserving material history.
- The tools for these operations and the user's preferred review and publication workflow within the skill's explicit approval boundary.

## Save and Revise

Use the configured representation and include content supported by the conversation. Preserve existing user-written context and material historical reasoning through focused updates, keeping parent relationships and dependencies consistent when stories change or move.

Record spike findings and their resulting epic and story updates so shaping can continue in another session without reconstructing the investigation. Verify that the requested writes and relationships succeeded before reporting persistence or spike closure as complete.
